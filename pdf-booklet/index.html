  3. Export and print

Page order is usually the difference between a document that makes sense and one that does not — a scan split across a duplex feeder, an export with the cover in the middle, a print job that wastes paper. PDF Booklet rearranges the pages visually, so you confirm the result before you export instead of guessing at a page range syntax.

PDF Booklet FAQ

How do I print it?

Double-sided, flip on the SHORT edge, no scaling (100%). Fold the stack in half and staple twice on the spine.

What is a signature?

The number of pages folded together. 16 pages is a comfortable booklet; a 200-page document becomes thirteen 16-page signatures.

Why did blank pages appear?

A signature must hold a multiple of four pages. Blanks are added at the end of the last signature — never in the middle, which would shift every page after the gap.

What is binding creep?

On thick booklets the inner pages sit further from the fold. Creep shifts the two halves outward by the millimetres you enter; leave it at 0 for thin booklets.

What do I get back after using PDF Booklet?

You get an imposed booklet PDF, ready to print double-sided and fold.

Do I need an account to use it?

No account, no email and no credit card. Open the page, add your file and download the result.

Is this safe for confidential documents?

Yes. The work happens locally, so no copy of your document is stored anywhere. There is no upload step to leak and no server-side queue to wait in.

Does it work on a phone, and can I use it offline?

Yes. The page works in any modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone, and once it has loaded it keeps working with no connection.
