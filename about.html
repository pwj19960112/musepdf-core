We believe PDF tools should be fast, private, and free.

No compromises.

Our Mission

To provide the most comprehensive PDF toolbox that respects your privacy and keeps its core tools free. We believe essential document tools should be accessible to everyone, everywhere, without barriers.

MusePDF is an independent website and tool collection; it is not affiliated with, or related to, MuPDF (the PDF library maintained by Artifex Software).

Our Core Philosophy

Privacy First. Always.

In an era where data is a commodity, we take a different approach. Local mode is our default: the 60 core tools process everything in your browser, so for those tools your files stay on your device and are not sent to a server. Cloud features, when you choose them, are the labelled exception.

Why MusePDF?

Built for Speed

No waiting for uploads or downloads to a server. By processing files directly in your browser using modern web technologies like WebAssembly, we offer unparalleled speed for all our tools.

Completely Free Core

No trials, no subscriptions and no hidden fees for the 60 core tools. They are a public utility, not a profit center.

No Account for Local Tools

Start using any of the 60 local tools immediately — no email, no password, no personal information.

Open Source Spirit

Built with transparency in mind. We leverage incredible open-source libraries like PDF-lib and PDF.js, and believe in the community-driven effort to make powerful tools accessible to everyone.

Ready to get started?

Join thousands of users who trust MusePDF for their daily document needs. Experience the difference that privacy and performance can make.
