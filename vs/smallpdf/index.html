MusePDF vs Smallpdf

Smallpdf is a hosted service: the file is uploaded, processed on their servers and downloaded again. MusePDF runs the same kind of job inside your browser tab. This page records that difference and the plan details that follow from it.

MusePDF and Smallpdf, row by row.
MusePDF Smallpdf
Where the file is processed Your own device, with JavaScript and WebAssembly On Smallpdf servers
Upload required No — the file is opened in your browser tab Yes — tools take the file over HTTP
Account needed No account, no email, no card A free plan and a 7-day Pro trial; the paid plans are account-based
Limits on the free tier None — no daily cap and no file-count cap The free plan is limited; Pro is sold as unlimited processing
Watermark on the free tier None Not stated on their pricing page
Paid tier None — the tools are free, and the source is Apache-2.0 Pro, monthly or annual, after a 7-day free trial. The price shown depends on region, so it is not repeated here — see their pricing page.
Tools covered All 60 local tools: merge, split, convert, edit, annotate, organise, optimise and secure A hosted set of PDF tools; their site lists the current ones
Works offline Yes, once the page has loaded No — the file has to reach their servers
Source code available Yes — Apache-2.0, in a public repository No

Facts checked November 2026. Competitor plans and limits change; verify on their site.

What we checked

We read smallpdf.com/pricing and the tool list on smallpdf.com on 20 November 2026. Smallpdf quotes a 7-day free trial and monthly or annual billing on that page.

Their pricing page · Their home page

When hosted processing is the right choice

A hosted service can do work a browser tab cannot: very large documents, server-side processing queues and shared team storage. If that is what you need, compare their plans on their site — this page only records what differs and leaves the choice to you.
