Invert Colors FAQ

What do I get back after using Invert Colors?

You get a dark-mode or inverted PDF.

Is this tool really free?

Yes. Every tool here is free with no account, no trial and no watermark. There is no daily limit and no paywalled export — the whole toolkit is open source.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Invert Colors runs in your browser with JavaScript and WebAssembly, so your one PDF are processed on your device and nothing is uploaded for this tool. That matters for contracts, invoices, IDs and anything under an NDA.

Does it work on a phone, and can I use it offline?

Yes. The page works in any modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone, and once it has loaded it keeps working with no connection.
